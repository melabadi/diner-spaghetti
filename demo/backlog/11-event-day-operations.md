## What's happening
The app stops being useful once the doors open. On the night we fall back to paper: ticking guests off at the door, working out who sits where, and writing up how it went afterwards.

## What I'd like
Three connected pieces, in this order, because each builds on the one before:

1. **Check-in.** Mark a reservation as arrived (fully or partially) and see arrived vs. expected guests at a glance.
2. **Table planning.** Assign reservations to tables, see which parties are still unseated, and respect notes such as "Deux tables côte à côte, si possible."
3. **Service report.** After the dinner, a summary of attendance and no-shows, money collected vs. still to collect, and volunteer slots filled.

## Notes
- This is too big for one pull request. I'd like to review it in layers.
- Reservations, volunteers and the budget must keep working exactly as they do today.
