---
title: "Stop booking a volunteer into overlapping shifts"
labels: ["enhancement"]
---
## What's happening
The volunteer form lets me sign the same person up for several shifts and tells me to check that the times are compatible myself. With the default shifts, *En cuisine* (15 h 30 – 18 h) and *Accueil & billets* (17 h 30 – 20 h) overlap, so it's easy to double-book someone by accident.

## What I'd like
The app should refuse, or at least clearly warn about, a sign-up that overlaps one of that volunteer's existing shifts.

## Notes
- Back-to-back shifts are fine: *Service aux tables* ends at 20 h 30 and *Rangement* starts at 20 h 30.
- Volunteers are identified by name only today.
