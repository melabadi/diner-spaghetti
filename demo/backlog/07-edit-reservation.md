## What's happening
On the Reservations tab I can add a reservation, mark it paid or pending, or delete it. That's all. When a family calls to say "we're now 8, not 6", or I mistype an email, the only option is to delete the reservation and enter it again, which also loses its payment status.

## What I'd like
An **Edit** action on each reservation row to change the contact name, email, adults, children, dietary counts and the note.

## Notes
- This comes up a few times a week while tickets are on sale.
- Editing must not let me oversell the room.
